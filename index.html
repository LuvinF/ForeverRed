To my Jannu,

My fourth-month letter

Four months with you.

I don't know how something can feel both so new and so familiar at the same time, but that's what you have become to me. You are still the girl who can make my heart race, but you're also becoming the person my heart feels safest with.

This fourth month gave me so many little memories I want to keep: the games, the kulfi, the movies, the funny jokes, the conversations that somehow meant everything reasoning as you speak.

And if you ever wonder what my favourite part was, it wasn't one particular game or one particular movie. It was you.

I enjoyed the moments because you were in them. I enjoyed the movies because I was watching them with you. I enjoyed the games because your laughter was somewhere inside them.

I love you when you're smiling, and I love you when you're annoyed. I love your softness and your fire. I love your calm days and your complicated ones. I don't need you to be perfect for me. I just want you to be you.

If these four months are only the beginning, then I hope we keep collecting these tiny worlds together — one laugh, one argument, one movie, one kulfi, one ridiculous, amazing and beautiful memory at a time.

You are not just a chapter I enjoyed.
You are the part of the story I want to keep writing.

Always yours,
